## Novelty and significance

The undulating mediastinal border of a non-gated helical chest CT records the beating
heart, and timing read out of it would let an existing scan be sorted by cardiac phase. That
begins with the period, and we give the sampling conditions it requires: two requirements whose
product is fixed by anatomy and reconstruction interval, plus a measured constant. On 192 real
series they are usually satisfiable. On images the evaluation is exploratory, no public series
recording a rate, and a criterion frozen in advance admits three fits to a nearly flat
objective: self-consistency does not establish a period.

## Ethical statement (for the form; not seen by reviewers)

This study used only publicly available, de-identified imaging distributed by The Cancer
Imaging Archive under Creative Commons licences, and generated no new patient data. No
institutional review board approval was sought, on the grounds that secondary analysis of
de-identified public data does not constitute human subjects research; approval for the
original acquisitions rests with the contributing institutions and is recorded by the
archive.
